Guardio — pros and cons

👍 The good

  • Blocks scam and phishing sites in real time, before they load.
  • Catches malicious browser extensions and downloads, not just bad pages.
  • Data-leak alerts for your email and phone number.
  • Up to $1 million in identity theft insurance on paid plans.
  • 24/7 email support, and an established company behind it since 2018.

👎 The trade-offs

  • It's a subscription — about $120 a year for one person at the yearly rate, every year.
  • Only works in Chrome and Edge on a desktop. If you live on your phone, it mostly isn't there.
  • Nobody reads your actual message. A strange text from a "new number" claiming to be your grandchild is exactly the kind of thing an automated site-blocker never sees.
  • No phone-number checker.
  • The 7-day trial asks for a card up front and turns into a paid subscription on its own — reviewers flag complaints about cancelling.
