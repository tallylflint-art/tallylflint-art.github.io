Is This Message a Scam?

Not sure if a message is a scam? Start with the question that matches yours — each page gives you a plain-English answer first, then what to do right now.

  • Is This Lottery Win or Prize Text a Scam? How to Tell Before You Pay Anything
    If you did not enter a lottery or sweepstakes, you did not win it.
  • Is This IRS Refund Email or Text a Scam? How to Tell a Fake IRS Message
    The IRS does not start a conversation about a refund or a tax debt by text, by email, or by a threatening phone call — and it never asks you to pay by gift card, wire transfer, or cryptocurrency.
  • Is This Package Delivery Text a Scam? USPS, UPS, and FedEx Texts Explained
    Delivery companies do not text you a payment link to release a package you are not expecting, and they do not ask you to "confirm" your address and card details through a link in a text.
  • Is This Bank Account Locked Text a Scam? How to Tell a Fake Bank Message
    A real bank will never text or email you a link and ask you to log in through it to unlock your account, and it will never ask you to move your money to a "safe account."
  • Is This Grandchild Emergency Call a Scam? AI Voice Clones and Family Emergency Scams
    A familiar voice is no longer proof.
  • Is This Text Message a Scam? How to Tell a Fake Delivery, Bank, or Toll Text
    If you were not expecting it, and it wants you to tap a link or pay in a hurry — treat it as a scam until you have proof it is real.
  • Check If a Phone Number Is Spam — Free Phone Number Spam Checker
    A strange number keeps calling or texting? Check it before you answer or call back — one-ring calls, premium numbers, and spoofed numbers are classic spam patterns.
  • Is This Email From Amazon or PayPal Real? How to Spot a Fake Brand Email
    Real Amazon and PayPal emails never ask you to log in through a link in the email, and never threaten to close your account in a few hours.
  • Is This a Romance Scam? Someone You Met Online Is Asking for Money
    If someone you met online — and have never met in person — asks for money, gift cards, or crypto, that is a romance scam.
