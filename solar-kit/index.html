Set up in minutes

  1. Set the panel where the sun hits it and plug it into the controller.
  2. Connect the controller to the battery — it starts charging.
  3. Clip the adapter onto the battery and plug in the inverter.
  4. Plug in your phone, lamp, or radio. That is the whole setup.
